CallGIF Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026

CallGIF is developed and operated by Bonehed Studios. This Privacy Policy explains how CallGIF accesses, uses, stores and shares information when you use the CallGIF Android application.

1. Information CallGIF Accesses

Contacts

CallGIF may request access to contacts stored on your device so that you can select contacts and assign CallGIFs to them.

Contact names and phone numbers are used locally on your device for CallGIF functionality. CallGIF does not upload your contact list to Bonehed Studios' servers.

Phone and Call Information

CallGIF functions as a phone application and may access information necessary to display and manage incoming and outgoing calls.

This information is used on your device to provide calling functionality and to determine which CallGIF should be displayed for a caller.

Bonehed Studios does not operate a server that stores your call history, contact information or telephone conversations.

2. GIF Search and GIPHY

CallGIF allows users to search for GIFs using GIPHY.

When you use the GIPHY search feature, the search terms you enter are transmitted to GIPHY so that relevant GIF search results can be returned.

This means that your in-app GIF search activity is shared with GIPHY when you choose to use the search feature.

CallGIF does not send the name or telephone number of the contact for whom you are selecting a GIF as part of the GIPHY search request.

Information processed by GIPHY is subject to GIPHY's own privacy practices and policies.

3. GIF Storage

When you assign a GIF to a contact, CallGIF may download and store a copy of that GIF locally on your device.

This allows assigned CallGIFs to load quickly and may allow them to work when an internet connection is unavailable.

These locally stored GIFs and CallGIF assignments are used to provide the app's functionality.

4. Information Bonehed Studios Collects

CallGIF does not currently require users to create an account.

Bonehed Studios does not currently operate a CallGIF backend service that collects or stores your contacts, telephone numbers, call history, GIPHY search history or assigned CallGIFs.

CallGIF does not currently use user data for advertising or marketing purposes.

CallGIF does not currently use third-party analytics or advertising services.

5. Data Sharing

CallGIF shares information with third parties only where necessary to provide functionality requested by the user.

When you choose to perform a GIPHY search, your search query is transmitted to GIPHY so that GIPHY can provide search results.

Bonehed Studios does not sell users' personal information.

6. Data Security

Network communications used by CallGIF for services such as GIPHY are transmitted using encrypted HTTPS connections.

Information that CallGIF stores locally is subject to the security protections provided by your Android device and operating system.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by CallGIF remains on your device as necessary to provide the app's functionality.

You can remove locally stored CallGIF data by changing or removing assignments where the app provides this functionality, clearing CallGIF's app data through Android settings, or uninstalling the application.

Search information transmitted to GIPHY may be processed or retained according to GIPHY's own policies and practices.

8. Children's Privacy

CallGIF is not specifically designed or directed at children.

Bonehed Studios does not knowingly operate a CallGIF service that collects personal information from children.

9. Third-Party Services

CallGIF uses GIPHY to provide GIF search and GIF content.

Third-party services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Users should review the privacy information provided by those services for further details about their practices.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as CallGIF develops or if the app's features or data practices change.

Any updated version will be published at this Privacy Policy's public web address with an updated effective date.
