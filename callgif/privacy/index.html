CallGIF Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

CallGIF is developed and operated by Bonehed Studios Ltd (company number 17568030), trading as Bonehed Studios. This Privacy Policy explains how the CallGIF Android application accesses, uses, stores and shares information.

1. Information CallGIF Accesses

Contacts

CallGIF may request permission to access contacts saved on your device so that you can recognise callers and assign animated GIFs to particular people. Contact names and phone numbers are used locally on your device. CallGIF does not upload your contact list to a Bonehed Studios server.

Phone and Call Information

CallGIF functions as a phone application and may access phone numbers, caller information and call state information needed to display and manage incoming and outgoing calls and choose the appropriate GIF. This processing occurs on your device. Bonehed Studios does not operate a server that stores your call history, contact information or telephone conversations.

Preferences and GIF Assignments

CallGIF stores your settings and the associations between phone numbers and GIFs locally on your device. You can change or remove assignments using the features provided by the App.

2. GIF Search and GIPHY

CallGIF uses GIPHY to provide GIF search and content. When you search for GIFs, the terms you enter are sent to GIPHY to return results. Your device also makes requests to retrieve GIF content, and GIPHY or its delivery providers may receive technical network information, such as your IP address, as part of those requests.

CallGIF does not intentionally include your contacts' names or phone numbers in GIF search requests. GIPHY's handling of information is governed by its own privacy policy.

3. GIF Storage and Device Transfers

When you download or import a GIF, CallGIF may save a local copy on your device. This helps assigned GIFs load promptly and may allow them to display without an internet connection.

Current versions of CallGIF use randomly generated filenames for app-managed GIFs, rather than including telephone numbers in filenames. GIF assignments and related app data remain stored locally.

CallGIF's configured backup rules exclude locally stored GIFs and assignment data from Android cloud backup, while allowing supported device-to-device transfer of these items. Actual transfer behaviour also depends on your device and Android's transfer facilities.

4. Subscriptions and Google Play

CallGIF may offer paid features through a subscription, including a free trial where available. Purchases, recurring payments, cancellations and refunds are handled through Google Play. Google processes payment and transaction information under its own privacy policy.

CallGIF may check purchase and subscription entitlement information provided through Google Play to determine access to paid features. Bonehed Studios does not receive your full payment-card details through the App.

5. Information Bonehed Studios Collects

CallGIF does not currently require a separate CallGIF account. Bonehed Studios does not currently operate a backend that collects or stores your contact list, call history, GIF assignments or GIPHY search history.

CallGIF does not currently include third-party advertising or analytics services, and Bonehed Studios does not sell users' personal information.

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and message, so that we can respond and provide support. Email service providers may process that correspondence on our behalf.

6. Purposes and Lawful Bases

CallGIF accesses information on your device to provide calling functionality, recognise contacts, display GIFs, remember assignments and settings, and check subscription access. The App uses third-party services to retrieve GIFs or support Google Play purchases as described above.

Where Bonehed Studios Ltd processes personal information subject to UK data protection law, we rely on the lawful basis appropriate to the activity. For example, handling support requests may be necessary for our legitimate interests in providing customer support, or to perform a contract with you where the request concerns a service we provide. We may also retain information where necessary to comply with a legal obligation, such as applicable business recordkeeping requirements.

7. Sharing and International Processing

GIPHY receives search queries and related network requests when you use GIF search or retrieval features. Google processes information associated with app distribution and Google Play purchases. These providers may process information outside the United Kingdom under their own arrangements and privacy notices.

We do not sell personal information. We do not transmit your complete contact list or call history to GIPHY or to a Bonehed Studios backend.

8. Data Security

CallGIF uses encrypted HTTPS connections when communicating with services such as GIPHY. Locally stored data benefits from the protections provided by your Android device and operating system. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Retention and Deletion

CallGIF's locally saved GIFs, assignments and preferences remain on your device until you change or delete them, clear the App's data or uninstall it, subject to any applicable device-transfer process. Uninstalling CallGIF does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

Information sent to GIPHY or Google is handled under those providers' retention policies. We retain emails sent to Bonehed Studios only for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with the enquiry, maintain relevant records or satisfy legal obligations.

10. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information held by Bonehed Studios Ltd; to restrict or object to certain processing; and to request data portability where applicable. These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

To exercise your rights or ask a question, email hello@bonehedstudios.com. You also have the right to raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

11. Children's Privacy

CallGIF is not specifically designed or directed at children. Bonehed Studios does not knowingly operate a CallGIF backend that collects personal information directly from children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as CallGIF or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at this address with a revised effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice of material changes.
